# Domain Playbook: Luxury & Independent Commerce

This playbook outlines the principles for designing independent, luxury, and artisanal e-commerce experiences. It rejects the hyper-optimized, anxiety-inducing patterns of fast fashion and mass commerce in favor of an editorial, story-driven approach.

## Anti-Patterns to Ban

*   **Fake Urgency:** No artificial countdown timers ("Sale ends in 5 minutes!").
*   **Gamified Capture:** No pop-up spin wheels or invasive "scratch to win" modals for email capture.
*   **Monolithic Pricing Grids:** No identical 3-card pricing or generic product grids.

## Recommended Patterns

*   **Tactile Material Close-ups:** Emphasize the physical qualities of the product through high-resolution, texture-focused imagery. Show the weave of the fabric, the grain of the leather, or the finish of the metal.
*   **Provenance Specification Tables:** Provide detailed information about where and how the product was made. Highlight the materials, the artisans, and the origin story.
*   **Editorial Pacing:** Structure the page like a magazine feature. Alternate between full-bleed imagery, negative space, and typographic storytelling. Allow the user to discover the product at their own pace.

## Narrative Focus

The primary goal is not immediate conversion through pressure, but building desire through appreciation of craft, quality, and story. The interface should feel like an extension of the product itself—thoughtful, refined, and enduring.
