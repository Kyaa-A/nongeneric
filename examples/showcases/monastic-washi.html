The Essence of Empty Space

In traditional Japanese aesthetics, Ma (間) translates to negative space, or the gap between structural elements. It is not merely an emptiness, but a dynamic spatial interval that gives shape to the whole.

Here, we explore the intersection of raw washi paper and deep sumi ink, creating an asymmetric editorial experience grounded in timeless typographic principles and deliberate pacing.
