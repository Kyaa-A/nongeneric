1998

The Analog Foundation

Before the digital acceleration, the structural principles were laid down in physical space. Architecture was bound by the constraints of gravity and material resistance.

2005

Digital Expansion

The network proliferated. Grids became fluid, and typography broke free from the static page. The screen became a stage for choreographed interactions.

2012

Algorithmic Synthesis

Code generated form. The boundary between designer and system blurred as parametric models dictated the evolution of structural aesthetics.
