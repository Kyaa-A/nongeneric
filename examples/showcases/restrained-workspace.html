DESIGN NOTE / 01

Let the work breathe.

Good tools give people a sense of direction. Every extra decision competes with the work they came to do.

One role, one treatment

Project names share a type size and weight. Supporting details share another. The hierarchy stays familiar as you move between the workspace and this reading view.

Motion has a job

This interface does not need an entrance sequence. Search responds immediately, navigation preserves a clear destination, and the settings dialog focuses the task at hand.

Details earn their place

Color marks useful states. Borders separate content. Space creates rhythm. The result can feel distinctive without asking the reader to admire the interface before using it.
