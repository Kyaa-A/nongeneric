Here begins the text, illuminated by rubrication and set in types that echo the hand of scribes. The parchment absorbs the ink, leaving a tactile impression of knowledge preserved through centuries.

The early printed books, known as incunabula, represent a transitional period where technology imitated manuscript traditions before finding its own mechanical voice.
